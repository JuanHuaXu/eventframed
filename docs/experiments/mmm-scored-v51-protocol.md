# V51: score-aligned outer head, prospective diagnostic

Research-only. Prior V49 full quality/cost failures remain authoritative.
No production, private corpus, whitepaper, commit or publishing change.
Loss/objective mismatch is a hypothesis; other explanations include weak
child recovery and poor scope routing. This experiment does not assume a cause.

[Vovk and Zhdanov's Brier algorithm](https://jmlr.org/papers/v10/vovk09a.html)
motivates the tested binary strong substitution. Keep the existing shared
Full/Adaptive/rich-moment2 children, member-local log head, and global log head
unchanged. Only the OUTER served/scored head varies in a frozen 2x2 factorial:
`log_mean`, `log_strong`, `brier_mean`, `brier_strong`. Original priors, all
three outer hazards, nomination clocks and original-advice ownership remain.

For original head advice p and revealed binary y, log emission is log(p) when
y=1 and log(1-p) otherwise. Brier emission is -2(p-y)^2. Unknown/canceled
positions retain unit emission and every original transition. Arithmetic
output is sum(w*p); strong output is the V50 binary substitution using the
AS-OF next log weights. The served receipt/snapshot retains this outer law,
not a post-hoc alternative used only for scoring.

These are predictive-strategy weights, not independent observations of one
joint Bayesian model. `log_strong` and `brier_mean` are factorial controls,
not claims of optimal loss mixability. Immediate, complete-feedback regret
does NOT automatically apply to delayed original-position replay, missing
labels, adaptive children, nested heads, selective observation or resets.
[Joulani et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v28/joulani13.html) explicitly
study delayed-feedback transformations; no inherited theorem is claimed here.

Scope: global/member caps 2400/16, 150 members, three child experts, two outer
heads, same 8 MiB constructor and 400 ms whole-loop screens. Complete issue,
resolve, snapshot and initial construction work counted. Original-position
suffix replay is bounded but NOT O(1): sorted insertion/replay can be linear
in admitted history, and an out-of-order complete trace can be quadratic.
No serving-tail/RSS guarantee follows from offline component cost.
