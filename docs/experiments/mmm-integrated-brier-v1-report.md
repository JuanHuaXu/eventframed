# Integrated Brier selector: rejected

The frozen four-expert selector pilot does not rescue downstream quality.
All seven research goals remain open. No runtime or whitepaper changes.

## Design

160 fresh independently fitted streams: two seed cohorts, five scenarios,
16 trajectories per cell, 4096 initial fit labels and 512 live frames each.
Compare the existing log-update/linear-forecast control with eta2 scalar-Brier
updates using either a linear forecast or strong binary Brier substitution.
All candidates use the control's actual observations; they cannot acquire hidden
features. Inner learner, fits, Anti-Pigeon decisions and split action are held
fixed. This is not a closed-loop observer-policy experiment.

The substitution follows Vovk and Zhdanov, *Prediction With Expert Advice For
The Brier Game*, Algorithm 1:
https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume10/vovk09a/vovk09a.pdf
Their summed two-outcome loss is twice scalar Brier. The one-step substitution
inequality is checked numerically; their static cumulative bound is not claimed
for the modified sharing and revocation process.

## Results

Post-period mean scalar Brier; lower is better. Intervals are paired trajectory
mean +/-3.5 standard errors, exploratory rather than anytime-valid guarantees.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Design | Member shift | .197033 | .201729 | .202432 | -.004697 [-.006590, -.002803] |
| Second | Member shift | .206759 | .212019 | .212688 | -.005261 [-.006379, -.004142] |
| Design | Common shift | .205702 | .210784 | .211401 | -.005082 [-.006058, -.004105] |
| Second | Common shift | .209584 | .214407 | .215110 | -.004823 [-.005726, -.003920] |
| Design | Recurring | .177839 | .183519 | .184120 | -.005680 [-.006847, -.004513] |
| Second | Recurring | .173265 | .179427 | .179941 | -.006162 [-.007076, -.005248] |

No cell passes the frozen .005 improvement plus positive lower-bound gate.
All primary .01 non-harm screens pass, but that wide tolerance does not erase
the consistent observed degradation. Stable and null effects are tiny.
Strong substitution improves on linear eta2 by about .0005-.0007 in changing
scenarios, insufficient to recover the existing selector's performance.

Loss alignment alone is not sufficient for this workflow. These results do not
falsify the paper's aggregation theorem or every Brier-based adaptive method.
Do not promote this implementation or tune eta/prior against these consumed
cohorts. Prefer a separately justified learner or task-evidence investigation.
