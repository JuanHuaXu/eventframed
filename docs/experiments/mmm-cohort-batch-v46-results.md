# V46 durable cohort batching result

2026-10-04. **Corrected full-workload correctness PASS; latency adoption FAIL
(0/16 control, 0/16 candidate).** Test-only eight-entry collection and native
commit caps are not deployed or adopted. Production and the whitepaper remain
unchanged. All seven whole research goals remain open.

## Preserved Failures

The first fork omitted three projected metadata methods. Receiver-method
inspection caught that before the experiment; original generated bytes and
mapping are preserved. The first checker self-test also failed because its VM
context lacked `structuredClone`; its failing source is retained. Neither is
reported as a scientific pass.

The first complete 32-trial workload in `research/cohort-batch-v46` then exposed
a more important dependency gap: workers collected more than four journals but
the inherited native commit adapters rejected them. Fifteen of sixteen candidate
trials fenced the store and failed to finish offered writes/outcomes. All sixteen
old control trials pass full correctness checks but fail latency. One candidate
happened to avoid the larger batch; it does not validate the design. The first
failed batch's explicit `[1,4]` error precedes stale-snapshot and missing-source
errors. This establishes the upstream transition, not merely its symptoms.
`failure-triage.json` retains all rows, rejections and first failed batches.
No apparent fast timing from those partial workloads is usable.

## Corrected Experiment

A new `native-cap` freeze forks both test-only native commit adapters. The
independent inverse AST test checks all 58 copied functions: exactly two worker
and two native size bounds change from four to eight, plus matching native error
messages and namespace renaming. No receipt, snapshot, provenance, projection,
validation, marker, publication, acknowledgement or cancellation predicates change.

An actual eight-journal FULL commit/reopen regression passes. Original adapters
still reject eight; candidate adapters reject nine. Ten candidate required test
roots and three original adjacent roots execute under race detection (not all
are native tests); validity/admission races, vet, streaming, owner-time and
process controls pass. Both cap declarations reject all 54 scientific corruption
variants, plus twelve positive/ten negative isolated cap-bound cases. The VM
checker changes only the batch bound and histogram in the inherited full audit.

The corrected load runs all 32 trials: two repetitions of future/visible writes,
joined/archive storage, eager off/on, cap four/eight. Cap order alternates by
repetition. Each completes 128 Recalls, 128 writes, 16 mixed outcomes and the
unchanged 150-nominee/eight-worker workload and arrival schedules. Every trial
passes the independent complete law/wire/epoch/lease/chain/returned-ack audit.
Source hashes remain unchanged through preflight, load and audit. The old prime
acknowledgement instrumentation gap remains explicit, not silently certified.

The candidate forms 184 batches larger than four. Total journal commits decrease
from 689 to 582 across sixteen trials per arm (all 2,064 journals per arm retained).
Every paired trial uses fewer commits. Outcome p99 improves in 15/16 pairs,
write p99 in 14/16, and offer-to-Recall p99 in 10/16; active-call p99 improves in
9/16 and worsens in 7/16. Both arms miss active-call 100ms once (102.58ms control;
101.10ms candidate), every offer/outcome 100ms gate and every outcome-max 250ms
gate. Writer p99 and used view-age maxima pass their 250ms gates in all trials.

Arithmetic means of **per-trial p99 values**, not pooled p99 or confidence bounds:
outcome 406.10ms control versus 359.33ms candidate; offer-to-Recall 230.25ms versus
223.54ms. Two repetitions, host scheduling variation and alternating order do
not establish population tail bounds or reliable non-inferiority. In particular,
archived visible eager Recall worsens in both repetitions. Increasing the batch
cap is useful component evidence, not a whole-contract rescue.

Future-only trials retain actual nonzero learned/transported corrections;
visible mutations retain zero under the original fail-closed epoch guard. This
is still an unresolved utility integration gap, not successful cross-epoch reuse.
