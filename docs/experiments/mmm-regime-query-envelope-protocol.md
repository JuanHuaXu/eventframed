# All-candidate query opportunity diagnostic

Frozen before collection. The original and disjoint-probe joint selectors do
not establish an advantage over random or entropy. This experiment asks whether
their eight-origin candidate pool contains useful alternatives. It is NOT a
deployable selector and cannot pass goal 7 by using hindsight.

Use all 2688 consumed v120 records, both schedules, every phase/case/index.
At clock 160 enumerate no-query (-1) followed by every unknown origin in
152..159, in ascending order. Do not inspect Y, Q, or future X to enumerate.
For each choice separately reveal only that paid label at 161, admit the same
natural arrivals, retain latest 64, and fit the unchanged segmentation model
(hazard .01, generic family mass .95). Forecast 161..191 with the existing
hazard aging. No sequential future learning is added. Each paid branch costs
one label, even when natural arrival at 161 makes it redundant.

Reuse the previously tested publication routine in groups of four branches,
padding the final group by repeating its final choice. Count the extra fits
from grouping and padding as research work; never describe them as serving cost.
Keep every branch, including no-query. Do not choose the best branch inside Go.

The offline scorer alone may use future Q to compute expected Brier. Report
the trajectory-wise minimum and maximum among paid choices, the minimum with
abstention allowed, uniform-over-pool expected loss, and each existing policy
(random, entropy, original joint, disjoint joint). A trajectory-wise oracle
uses future truth and is an optimistic bound, not evidence of online learnability.
Report all 84 phase/case/schedule cells, 32-trajectory descriptive paired
mean +/- 3.5 SE intervals, and the number of trajectories with any paid choice
better than no-query. Do not call these simultaneous or anytime intervals.

Before collection, verify ownership, detached/concurrent replay, unchanged
original-policy forecasts, and per-branch exclusion of unrevealed labels and
future Y/Q. Verify invalid source lengths are rejected. The scorer must check
complete candidate enumeration, support/cost/redundancy accounting, aging,
and exact forecasts against both previous artifacts for their selected arms.
Hash sources and code, retain errors, and replay the summary byte-for-byte.

No production, whitepaper, or whole-goal promotion. If opportunity is material,
the next question is whether it is predictable from predecision evidence, not
whether hindsight selection itself is successful acquisition.
