## Hypothesis And Prior Evidence

V34 learned dispersion in stationary independent member rates, helping specific
low-variance cases but failing its full screen. V35 helped particular two-rate
shapes and also failed overall. V70 proved that unconditional prior widening
can increase response while increasing one-observation stationary excess risk.
Those facts motivate a model comparison; they do not uniquely diagnose V69's
failure or prove this integration will rescue it.

[Gelman (2006)](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/bayescomputation/Gelman2006.pdf)
explains hierarchical variance-prior sensitivity and partial pooling in a
normal model; it does not prescribe these Bernoulli grids. [Adams and MacKay
(2007)](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.3742v1) distinguishes observation models from
changepoint priors. Here the dispersion family is STATIC. Member reset dynamics
do not make the dispersion hyperparameter a changepoint-adaptive process.

## One Declared Joint Law

Before observing any new experiment outputs, fix equal prior probabilities on
three family alternatives: baseline point mass; the current 0.8 point mass plus
0.2 finite urn prior of strength 1; and a no-spike finite urn prior of strength
2. The finite urn draws 20 times and supports rates j/20. Each family's mean
is the supplied baseline b. This is a discrete law, not identical to V34's
continuous Beta integrations. Noise takes 0, 0.1, 0.2 with probabilities
0.8, 0.1, 0.1. No retrospective prior fitting is allowed.

Conditional member rates follow T(z,z') = (1-hazard) I(z=z') + hazard*pi(z'),
with hazard 1/16 per MEMBER issue ordinal, not wall time or evidence arrival.
Latent Y is Bernoulli(rate). W1 and W2 are conditionally independent noisy
measurements of the SAME Y. A pair replaces the original first-only likelihood;
it is not an additional independent outcome. Pending, missing and cancelled
measurements supply no evidence. Epoch transitions reset posterior and ownership.

Three sharing structures: common family/common noise (learn); common family
and independent member noise (local); independent member families AND noise
(individual). Individual ModelWeights is only a descriptive mean of the member
family posteriors, not a common posterior. Shared hyperparameters do not equate
member outcomes and do not authorize an Anti-Pigeon merge.

For common family/common noise, posterior odds are proportional to prior times
the product of conditional member evidence. Local noise is marginalized before
the common-family evidence product. Individual uses each member's evidence only.
Issued clean and measured predictions come from those SAME posterior weights.
Paired query values include any nonlocal movements implied by the declared law.

Family classification has 3 classes; family/noise classification has 9. Expected
class concentration gain is evaluated independently by actual branch posteriors.
Latent falsification uses the same Gini objective as V69, evaluated by its stable
covariance identity; tiny tie differences from subtractive arithmetic are not
claimed bitwise identical. No EC2 competitiveness or external-truth guarantee.
