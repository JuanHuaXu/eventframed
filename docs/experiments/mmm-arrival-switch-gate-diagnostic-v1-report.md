Conditional TV is the uniform-input average of total variation between the
old and new Bernoulli outcome laws, evaluated from synthetic truth. It is an
evaluator quantity, not an estimated runtime certificate. For independent
uniform input streams, correctness indicators instead have Bernoulli laws
whose TV equals the absolute accuracy gap. Thus this projection loses much
of the available distributional contrast in the reverse direction.

The reverse mean signed gap is .126123 versus .45 forward. Individual reverse
gaps range from -.0421875 to .309375. None is exactly zero; the hypothesis that
these runs were completely invisible is falsified. Three reverse trajectories
have absolute gaps below .03. Merely waiting longer would not repair the loss
of signal efficiently, and this diagnostic supplies no valid lower threshold.

First-cohort reverse mean accuracy changes .744336 to .497363, with5/16 gate
allows,12/16 Bayesian nominations, and5/16 splits. Its forward case again has
16/16 splits. This is not just a one-cohort coincidence, but the two cohorts
remain exploratory evidence rather than fresh confirmation.

## Exact Trace Replay

For all256 schedules, the diagnostic:

- reconstructs the frozen base from the declared seeds and checks its issued
  bounded-view probabilities against every raw live/reference monitor value;
- enumerates512 inputs against both latent rules with the5% label-noise law;
- replays actual arrival, missing-label expiry, and ordered evidence release;
- runs the unchanged Bayesian monitor and paired external investigator;
- checks every released origin and the first combined split clock against the
  original experiment, recording nominations, external permission, and wealth.

The seven-versus-one nomination/permission result therefore identifies the
external evidence gate as a limiting step in these reverse runs. It does not
prove that bypassing it would improve forecasts. Stable nominations were
correctly blocked; weakening the guard would discard a demonstrated benefit.
