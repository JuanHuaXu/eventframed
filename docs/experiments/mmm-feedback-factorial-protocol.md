# Delay versus missingness factorial

Freeze before quality outcomes. Use all1344 latent consumed v120 trajectories,
whose Initial and X/Y/Q have already been paired across schedules. Four arms:
immediate/complete; immediate/original missing mask; original delays/complete;
original delays/original missing mask. No paid acquisition. Original four expert
fitters and Markov selector,32-frame publication and64/32-count caps unchanged.

Use delay/missing flags as exogenous generator interventions, not a deployable
ability to see missing labels. Release truth only by each arm's declared schedule.
Refit experts; never reuse predictions from a different arm. Already verified
original complete/immediate and delayed/missing outputs may be reused after
identity/hash checks; validate new harness against those controls before scoring.

For every phase/case/window/expert, report4 Briers and paired contrasts: delay
cost with complete feedback; missingness cost with zero delay; interaction
combined-minus-delay-only-minus-missing-only-plus-complete. Their sum equals the
total delayed/missing penalty. Negative costs/interactions are allowed. Intervals
mean +/-3.5SE over32 trajectories, exploratory not simultaneous. All1344 pairs;
no seed tuning or fresh claims.

Verify source/input hashes, no current/future Y/Q leakage, natural controls,
decomposition identity and replay. Retain new-arm per-expert predictions,
metrics and fit-origin lists. This selects a research direction, not a new
success criterion or a reason to weaken the seven goals' requirements.
