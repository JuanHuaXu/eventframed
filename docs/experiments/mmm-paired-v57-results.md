## Research And Implementation

The decision-utility perspective follows Chaloner and Verdinelli (1995),
[primary author report](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/tr/tr599/tr599.html).
Selecting observations for parameter identification need not optimize prediction.
Our finite binary identity is separately derived, not inherited from the report's
normal-linear-model results.

For next-issue clean targets, the observer scores the expected weighted squared
movement of their conditional means after a second measurement of the ORIGINAL
outcome. Under one current joint model, the tower identity makes this equal the
expected reduction in Bayes Brier risk. Uniform target weights 1/150 are frozen.
This is model utility, not external truth, an optimal batch rule, or delay-aware
value. No generator rates, future labels, or hidden source availability enter
the observer. Conditional evidence is hypothetical and never committed.

Isolated package `internal/researchpairedvalue` clones the V56 learner without
changing its forecast law. It evaluates original-position hypothetical factor
histories, retaining member-local effects and changed global weights. The new
reference `internal/researchpairedvalueref` independently sums unnormalized
histories and subtracts expected proper risk rather than copying the observer's
squared-movement computation. No production integration was enabled.

missing-response stresses are included, not excluded after observing results.

interval, fresh scientific confirmation, or evidence of agent-task improvement.
The unused design/confirmation seeds and sealed outcome-labeled tasks remain

## Performance

Prediction-value proposal work totals 32.264 seconds across 120 arms. Complete
loop cost is 125.87% higher than random, 36.55% higher than uncertainty, 34.47%
higher than information, and 35.75% higher than concentration in the same run.
All 120 predictive cells fail 400 ms. Constructor allocation is 3,625,760 bytes,
below 8 MiB, but this is not RSS or total query allocation.

The separate initial microbenchmarks measured 150-origin dense batches at
7.4-7.7 ms with one issued trial/member, about 15.3 ms at eight, and 23.2-23.5 ms
at sixteen, allocating about 307 KB per batch. A small isolated batch time did
not imply complete-loop success. Setup, target construction, integrations,
normalization, sorting, request forecasts, resolutions and draining are charged
in the full experiment. No loaded 100 ms serving or freshness claim.

Fixed-order shared-host timings remain a limitation. V56's one falsification
cost failure becomes zero here, without a law or arithmetic change; do not use
that variation to erase the earlier failing result or call a new speed rescue.
Scientific risk differences have no repeated-population uncertainty intervals.

## Consequence For The Next Lead

The finite mathematical counterexample survives: concentrated model-class belief
can coexist with useful local information. The stronger practical hypothesis
does not survive this diagnostic: changing observation utility alone cannot
repair forecast-model mismatch, source-dependence errors, or delayed evidence.

Useful remaining leads are bounded model-error protection and an adaptive
predictive challenger, plus amortized predictive-value computation if justified
by subsequent quality evidence. Cost reduction alone would not repair the broad
quality failures. Immediate expected utility must not be presented as a
latency-aware value certificate. Fresh equal-total-cost and untouched agent-task
studies remain necessary; this negative does not authorize retuning sealed tasks.
