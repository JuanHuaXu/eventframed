# Label-budget curves V33: frozen protocol

Frozen2026-10-03 before either outcome split. All seven whole goals OPEN.
Production, whitepaper, older studies and tracked changes stay untouched.

## Fresh Worlds and Nomination

Two cosine geometries, twelve unchanged V30 regimes and32 worlds per cell
give768 worlds per split. Seed bases2026103303 design and2026103304
confirmation. World seed=base+geometry*100000000+regime*1000000+world*1000;
truth/label/nomination offsets101/202/303 unchanged. This geometry stride
avoids cross-cell seed collisions. Do not reuse V31/V32 outcomes.

Six learners: local baseline-centred Beta, affine, partition, ordinary
family blend, original-row prequential stack, exact LOO crossfit. The
unupdated baseline is a separate static forecast repeated at each budget.
Every learner receives the SAME150 distinct members/outcomes in the same
label-independent randomized-stratum order. Retain full original Q6 and
stack-issued child rows, plus snapshot laws/weights/LOO rows.

Checkpoints16,32,64,128,150 are fixed. One observed label per member;
new future Bernoulli outcomes at the same fixed member rate define the
risk. Evaluating a future expectation is not resubstitution on training
labels. No temporal shift, missing/delayed labels or agent answer task.

## Fixed Decisions and Gate

Models and ridge/priors stay fixed. Report every model's whole/priority
Brier, top10 usefulness/Brier/bias at every budget. Observed/unobserved
future Brier have declared denominators L and150-L; unobserved at150 is
null, NOT zero or evidence of extrapolation success. Include weights and
measured costs rather than select a best budget from consumed outcomes.

The information-scarcity-alone hypothesis is screened at PREDECLARED150
labels using the V32 quality rule for crossfit:

- Protect local, affine, blend and stack on whole/priority Brier and
  packet usefulness: paired gain lower>=-.01.
- Curved/shifted_peak improve over affine: whole/priority mean gain>=.01
  and lower>0; usefulness mean>=.02 and lower>0.
- Wide-independent/wide-curved improve over blend at those .01/.02
  thresholds and positive lower endpoints.
- Packed bias abs(mean)+3.5SE<=.10 in every cell.
- Zero audit/duplicate/future/source/LOO/law errors; maximum per-model
  measured AccountedNS<=10ms. AccountedNS is not wall/serving latency.

Every requirement must pass in BOTH splits to support scarcity-only
rescue on these finite known families. Failure keeps that hypothesis
rejected; it does not establish which alternative explanation is true.
Other budgets are descriptive diagnostics, NOT after-the-fact adoption
gates. Mean+/-3.5SE intervals use32 independent worlds per cell. They are
not confidence sequences or simultaneous AP certificates. Paired curves
within worlds are dependent and must not be counted as new trajectories.

## Reproducibility

Exclusive-create both raw tapes and freeze19 source/test/protocol/checker
hashes. Independently reconstruct the sampler's nonempty bucket/probability,
all six pre-outcome laws, original stack rows, prefixes, full future laws,
exact LOO rows, fit weights via an analytic edge/interior solver, static
baseline, split-population denominators, packets, risk and cost sums.
Full Go seed replay checks all non-timing fields for both splits. Unit
tests flip labels that have not arrived at a checkpoint and compare exact
prefix state/laws. Include negative auditor controls without editing tapes.

Matched-label volume does NOT equate total acquisition/compute cost across
budgets or complete Goal7. Model timing does not establish loaded learning
freshness/serving. No publication, deployment or change to old gates.
