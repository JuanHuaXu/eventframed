## Arms and Chronology

Retain all eleven V30 controls and policies on these fresh worlds. Add four
stack arms: random-within-stratum (primary), uniform random, entropy, and
the .2-exploration child-disagreement heuristic. Each non-baseline arm
gets32 distinct outcomes. The primary's indices, outcomes, and conditional
probabilities MUST equal local/affine/partition/V30-blend stratum controls.

Stack child means are sealed with an Issue ticket BEFORE the selected
outcome is read. Its trace stores the issued stack mean, probability,
selection score, and all three issued child means. Resolve uses its private
row; after resolution fit weights and update child posteriors. On these
immediate streams no pending labels remain at the next nomination. Pending
and delayed-ticket semantics are tested separately, not extrapolated to
delayed recovery or asynchronous serving.

The optimization, anchor (.98,.01,.01), SUM-loss ridge coefficient1, child
families, and exploration floor are fixed as in the preflight. Predictive
weights are NOT family probabilities, and disagreement is NOT Bayesian
