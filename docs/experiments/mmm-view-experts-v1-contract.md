# Separately scored view experts: consumed-tape diagnostic

Hypothesis, not a confirmed fix: weights trained on narrow-view advice may not
transfer to the already-paid expanded view. Competing explanations are weak
expanded-view models, delayed feedback, and acquisition/model coupling. Test a
separate selector over the two COMPLETE forecast laws, not new weights within
either law. A failed recovery/nonharm screen falsifies this bounded rescue.

Use the verified available-evidence tape and credit-learning parent, all128
trajectories and both schedules. No new seeds or confirmation claim. Freeze
acquisition, model fits, inner/outer component weights and Anti-Pigeon clocks.
Advice is [original, available, available, available] in the existing ForecastMix:
prior .7/.3 aggregate mass, share .002, clipped Bernoulli likelihood, unit weight.
Duplicated slots are one alternative with .3 prior mass, NOT independent evidence.
This is fixed-share expert aggregation, not an ordinary Bayesian joint posterior.

At each clock issue the forecast FIRST, then deliver nonmissing labels in stable
origin order. Journal advice at issuance; never reconstruct it on delivery.
Arrival delay is0..31. Missing labels do not update. Flush through543. Both view
roles persist across component model publications and splits, so carry selector
history and accept late advice from before those transitions. No split-triggered
reset or retrospective retiming. This does not reuse a residual certificate.

Primary comparison: selected view law versus original arm2 law. Also retain the
unconditional available-view endpoint. Reverse-shift gain requires mean>=.005
and mean-3.5SE>0 in allfour cohort/schedule cells. Full/post harm upper bounds
must be<=.01 in all16 cells. SE uses16 trajectories, not frames. These are consumed
exploratory screens, not simultaneous confidence sequences. No parameter sweep.

Verify future-label isolation, missing-label isolation, stable same-clock
delivery, equal-advice identity, bounded advice validation, deterministic replay,
and independent JavaScript/Go selector agreement before interpreting scores.
Capture input/source hashes and per-frame forecasts/weights. Measure selector
arithmetic separately from full service; fixed tape excludes fitting, acquisition,
database I/O and policy feedback. A pass would require subsequent closed-loop
testing and untouched confirmation; a failure is retained without adoption.
