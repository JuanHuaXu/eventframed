success or regret theorem. Event-window substitution improved some raw models
but harmed complete forecasts. Competing explanations: scarce support, incorrect
old weights, error correlation and feedback delay. Test the weight hypothesis
without altering fitted data, acquisition or Anti-Pigeon permission.

event64 subset, event64 subset]. Existing ForecastMix prior .7/.1/.1/.1 and
share.002 remain unchanged; duplicated subset slots have .2 total prior, not
independent evidence. An independent outer ForecastMix uses [original base,
new inner mixture, original pooled/local, neutral]. Both learn the advice
actually issued at origin, at nonmissing outcome arrival with unit weight.

Forecast before same-clock feedback; stable origin ordering for batches. Apply
the ORIGINAL external split at the end of its clock, after feedback. Original
outer and candidate outer use existing coupledArrivalRevoke; never reinterpret
its pre-normalization .1 cap as a persistent normalized cap. Inner view/window
roles persist. As in the parent, after split reject feedback origins<=split clock.
No retrospective reset or true regime-change clock supplied to the learner.

captured original weights/laws (tolerance1e-12); its available-view comparison

separately. This is not a closed-loop changed-acquisition model or independent
available-view baseline training.
