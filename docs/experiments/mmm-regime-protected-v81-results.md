## Two important negatives

Cap9 with protected resets forces reset-only trajectories and loses memory.
The limiting-control test verifies this rather than presenting it as learning.
Positive exact-arithmetic evidence does not establish useful approximation or
an informative lower bound over long histories.

with true positive probability: an independent class-mixture log oracle gives
logP=-1321.2095576498039. Linear filtering rejects that observation through

and NOT repaired by this support policy. Log-domain stored component weights
