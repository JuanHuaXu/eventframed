## Scope And Hypothesis

production change or quality rescue. V71 remains immutable. Repeated posterior
marginalization and next-state normalization are present in issue, nomination,
and snapshot paths. They are plausible runtime contributors, not a unique
profiling diagnosis of the quality failures. Cache derived objects when their
source beliefs are published. Preserve the exact arithmetic and scored law.

For common noise, cache global joint odds with explicit zero-support counts.
For independent noise, cache each member's noise marginal and conditional odds.
Use ordered publication-time rebuilding, not subtraction of infinite logs.

and hypothetical branches publish nothing. Cancellation changes no evidence.
Epoch changes construct a fresh model. Public numeric fault detection remains;
the cheap log guard is not a certificate against arbitrary private-memory edits.
