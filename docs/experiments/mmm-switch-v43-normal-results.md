## Next lead, not a validated rescue

Specialist expert routing is a source-backed alternative to one global mixer
weight state. [Freund et al. (1997)](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~yfreund/papers/SpecializedExperts.pdf)
restrict prediction and updates to awake specialists, leaving other weights
unchanged. [Herbster et al. (2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07055) study multitask
online learning with long-term memory. These motivate testing context-local
routing alongside global evidence borrowing. Their bounds do not automatically
prove Brier non-inferiority, delayed adaptive-expert validity or Anti-Pigeon
sharing. Local routing may improve partial shifts but lose sample efficiency.
A prospective formulation, independent reference, total cost bound and fresh
cohorts are required; it has not been implemented or selected from this result.

This advances the diagnostic evidence for Goals 1/2/4, not completion. Goal 3
needs valid splitting/downstream tests, Goal 5 untouched agent task improvement,
Goal 6 loaded freshness/latency, and Goal 7 equal-total-cost observation wins.
Production, private corpora and whitepaper remain untouched. All seven whole
goals remain open and the persistent goal remains active.
