# V57 prediction-targeted observation protocol

## Hypothesis And Scope

Recommendation, not a bug fix: future prediction risk may be a better observation
objective than uncertainty in the coarse shared model class. Implement expected
reduction of next-issue clean binary Brier risk using ONE current joint model.
Uniform target weights 1/150 are frozen. No use of generator rates, hidden
labels, private corpora, source availability, or future target outcomes in the
observer. Immediate model utility is not delay-aware utility or external truth.

Primary inspiration: Chaloner and Verdinelli (1995),
[author report](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/tr/tr599/tr599.html). This supplies the
decision-utility perspective, not a theorem establishing EventFrame's gains.
The finite binary proper-risk identity is derived and tested separately.

eight arms = 960 arms. New predictive-value selection plus unchanged Full,

falsification controls. Original RNG namespace and diagnostic seed 2026105407.
Design 2026105409 and confirmation 2026105411 remain UNEVALUATED. Diagnostic n=1
per cell, no confidence intervals or independent confirmation claim.

Each arm issues 2,400 first measurements. The five selection policies request
25 second measurements after all first observations of each 150-member round
arrive, totaling 2,800 requested measurements per arm. W1 and W2 measure the
SAME original latent Y. Requested missing responses remain in cost/accounting;

All 18 original regimes plus two 20%-missing-source stresses remain. Correlated
source-noise and delay cases cannot be excluded when reporting results. A batch
is selected before any same-tick second outcome is revealed; descending values
use stable member-order ties. Myopic utility is evaluated at the as-of posterior,
including later revealed observations, through original-slot factor replacement.
No adaptive within-batch lookahead or greedy optimality claim.

## Decision Rules

Retain the earlier 0.01 Full-relative average loss gain and maximum 0.01
Adaptive-harm criteria, recovery/usefulness comparisons, and all costs. Report
comparisons against no-pair, random, uncertainty, information, and concentration.
The new prediction utility intentionally may change selections; fixed-evidence
law invariance is verified through unchanged-control comparisons and independent
replay. Tiny gains cannot substitute for broad scientific gates.

Equal request count is NOT equal total cost. A lower Brier result with higher
compute does not close Goal 7; no equal-total-cost superiority is established by
this diagnostic. No external agent-task evidence, Anti-Pigeon split certificate,
source-authentication guarantee, loaded 100 ms claim, or whole-goal closure.

confirmation is not dispatched merely to search for favorable outcomes.
