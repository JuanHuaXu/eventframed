## Background-Merge Lead

[FreshDiskANN](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.09613v1), especially section5, separates
recent mutable state from a long-term graph and merges in the background. Its
reported scale/latencies do NOT transfer to this small HNSW wrapper. Relevant
design lesson: bounded changed-state storage and merge rate/backpressure must
be accounted for; low individual insertion cost is not sustained throughput.

Prototype a frozen read-only overlay plus bounded new tail, one compactor and
immutable generation publication. Build from committed watermark a; retain and
rebase EVERY intervening committed mutation through publication watermark b.
Wal failure, deletion/upsert ordering, cancellation/close, readers of retired
generations, query-time availability and source epochs remain mandatory. Never
let a maintenance-only swap change which durable writes are acknowledged. If
the tail fills, explicit backpressure/error is preferable to silent truncation.
Measure construction cost and transient/retired bytes, not just pointer-swap time.
