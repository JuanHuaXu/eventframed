# Dynamic Dispersion After Class Observation

Recommendation only; not an implemented runtime rescue or a completed goal.
The V69 experiment is undergoing independent replay. Its raw summaries show
small class-observation gains but continued losses against the strong controls.
Changing which repeated observation is selected has not removed the larger
learner-quality gap. The V70 finite check separately shows why unconditional
prior widening can buy faster response at the cost of greater stationary error.

## Related Evidence, Not A New Claim

V34 already implemented coherent, stationary dispersion learning with shared
hyperparameters and independent member rates. It improved low-variance cases
and remained a failed overall screen. V35 added bounded member-rate shapes and
repaired specific two-rate cases, also failing the whole screen. Their models,
outcomes, thresholds and failed artifacts must remain unchanged. This proposal
is an integration of those existing component leads, not a novel hierarchy.

[Gelman (2006)](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/bayescomputation/Gelman2006.pdf)
discusses sensitivity to hierarchical variance priors and learning hyperparameters.
Its normal-model recommendations do not prescribe our finite Bernoulli family
or establish its protection. [Adams and MacKay (2007)](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.3742v1)
separates the segment observation model from the changepoint prior; coherent
static learning is not by itself adaptive recovery.

## Required Isolated Model

Declare a fixed hyperfamily over the baseline-point and member-rate dispersion/
shape alternatives. Each hyperstate must define ONE complete joint model for
the original Y, first/second measurements, member-rate dynamics and noise.
Members may retain independent rates conditional on a shared hyperstate;
sharing a dispersion parameter is not equating their outcomes or authorizing
an Anti-Pigeon merge. No evidence may tune the prior retrospectively.

Compute hyperstate evidence from its member likelihoods, and combine the SAME
hyperstate posteriors into the issued clean and measured laws. Paired evidence
replaces the original trial factor; it does not add an independent outcome.
Define whether hyperparameters are static or evolve, and give member/global
issue clocks explicitly. Late evidence must replay those clocks, not reset on
arrival or manufacture a new trial. Any finite discretization is its own law,
not silently identical to V34's continuous Beta integrals.

## Gates Before A Full Experiment

1. Independently enumerate a small joint model, including common noise,
   dispersion weights, nonlocal evidence effects and both measurement branches.
   Test zero support, prior means, tower identity and arbitrary latent refinement.
2. Reproduce limiting stationary components where the declarations truly match;
   document discretization differences instead of claiming exact equivalence.
3. Implement in new packages. Audit delayed/future boundaries, atomic publication,
   epochs/caps, noise underflow revival, and receipts before collecting outcomes.
4. Retain current, no-sharing, no-pair, Full and Adaptive controls on the complete
   consumed diagnostic. Preserve every failed cell, not only favorable shifts.
   Charge all nomination, replay, fitting, memory and evidence costs.
5. Only a useful controlled result warrants fresh broad replication and an actual
   equal-TOTAL-cost observation study. No reserved seeds or sealed agent labels
   are opened during model development.

This does not replace the other work: Goal3 still needs useful valid splits,
Goal5 untouched outcome-labeled agent tasks, and Goal6 loaded durable freshness
with visible mutations and recovery. All seven original whole success criteria
remain active, with production, publication and private data untouched.
