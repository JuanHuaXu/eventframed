## Second: Mean Calibration, Not Only Dispersion

All three V71 priors share each member's baseline mean. [V34's prior work](../docs/experiments/mmm-dispersion-v34-results.md)
jointly modeled mean and dispersion and had specific positive components,
although its overall screen failed. [Gelman (2006)](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/bayescomputation/Gelman2006.pdf)
supports scrutiny of hierarchical priors, not the selection of our future grid.
[Adams and MacKay (2007)](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.3742v1) reinforces that an
observation family and its change dynamics must be specified separately.

Finite preflight first: an explicit bounded mean-calibration family, separate
member rates and independent/common noise alternatives. Use the SAME joint
model in likelihood, issued forecast and observation values. Do not apply an
arbitrary fitted calibration map to the forecast while leaving its likelihood
unchanged. Declare whether any field is static; do not label static evidence
accumulation adaptive regime recovery. Do not assume identifiability against
noise: enumerate same-Y paired responses and distinct-outcome evidence.

Choose the grid/priors before collecting new outcomes, and charge grid width.
Finite grids are not V34's continuous integrations. Preserve a baseline/current
control and prior-mean tests for whichever initial law is actually declared.
Check nonlocal effects, support, tower and branch utility independently. Reuse
grid-atom likelihoods where mathematically identical, not the baseline atom
when its calibrated mean differs. Memory, fitting, nomination and publication
must meet the original caps; any approximation gets an explicit error boundary.

If useful on the full consumed screen, freeze broader independent replication
before opening ANY reserved seeds or sealed agent outcomes. If not useful,
retain its failure and reconsider dynamics/representation/observation scarcity.
This does not displace all seven goals: useful valid Anti-Pigeon splitting,
untouched agent utility and durable loaded freshness remain active work.
