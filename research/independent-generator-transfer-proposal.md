# Independent-generator transfer: next research priority

Status: generator implemented and scoped QA passed; see the
[construction and verification contract](independent-generator-contract.md).
The frozen learner comparison protocol and quality experiment are still
required. This advances roadmap direction1 and tests whether the useful portions
of directions2/4 generalize. It does not rescue or erase the failed snapshot
candidate, and it does not replace the remaining requirements in directions3/5/6/7.

## Why now

V115 rejects the bounded snapshot strategy under the unchanged complete quality
criteria, including the matched gate-budget control. Earlier v109-v114 results
largely share the same Boolean truth/input helpers. Fresh seeds establish new
samples, not independence from that generator construction. The inspected v83/v88
breadth protocols also use bit/parity/majority/multiplexer cases. The separate
public sparse-logistic pilot is not a soft-response generator transfer experiment.

Before another weighting mechanism, evaluate the unchanged generic, arrival-log
and fixed-Markov controls on independently implemented truth families. Do not
silently call the existing strongest controls fully validated; they still fail
some original recovery requirements.

## Generator families to implement and verify

1. Soft additive response: Bernoulli probability from a bounded logistic function
   of a small signed subset of coordinates, with non-extreme conditional noise.
2. Hierarchical response: a decision-list/tree construction with independently
   assigned leaf probabilities and branching variables, not a parity alias.
3. Random local interaction table: independent non-degenerate probabilities over
   a small relevant-coordinate table, rather than a hand-selected Boolean rule.

These are new synthetic constructions, not claimed reproductions of a research
paper or substitutes for real agents. Write their generators without calling the
old truth helpers. Independent exhaustive512-input checks should verify declared
means, probability bounds, relevant/irrelevant coordinates and conditional
averages before model outputs are inspected. Avoid accidental oracle, task-name
or generator-ID features in the learner interface.

Preserve uniform input measure initially to isolate response-family transfer;
dependent input-law transfer remains a separate required experiment. Restrict the
relevant variables to the same declared acquisition budget. Do not give a learner
the true relevance set or true conditional law. Full training audits must remain
explicitly charged and equal across arms.

## Required temporal designs

Include stationary, abrupt response changes and gradual mixtures between two
response laws. Predeclare change-time ranges not all aligned to publication
boundaries. Withhold change time and regime identity from the policy. Keep the
same missing/delayed schedules initially, followed by separate coverage of other
feedback laws. Separate prior fitting samples from scored trajectories.

Before generation, freeze every coefficient/leaf distribution, phase split,
stream length, fit cadence, teacher support, seed range, sample count, metric
segment and comparison gate. Validate effective-seed separation from all archived
experiments. No pilot output may become the new confirmation set.

## Evaluation boundary

Keep expected Brier primary and paired trajectory-level comparisons, with honest
uncertainty and unchanged baseline non-harm tolerance. Recovery gain tests must
be declared for every changed family before outcomes, not chosen from favorable
cells afterward. Derive explicit counts of mandatory gates in the new protocol.
Different intrinsic noise means raw accuracy cannot be compared to the earlier
95% ceiling; report each teacher's Bayes Brier floor and accuracy ceiling as
diagnostics only. They cannot drive the forecast or replace the measured policy.

A transfer failure is useful evidence about the spec's learning assumptions.
It is not permission to shrink the task to easy stationary cases. A transfer pass
would still leave the original unresolved gates, actual prospective tasks,
Anti-Pigeon diameter/coverage, and loaded durable serving requirements open.
