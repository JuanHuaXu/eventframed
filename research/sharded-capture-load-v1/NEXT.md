# Required Follow-Up, Not Completion

The original seven-goal requirements are unchanged. This pilot targets a loaded
serving bottleneck, not a substitute for science or real-task improvement.

1. Replicate the final repaired candidate on a new controlled large-corpus scale
   ladder with the same full frontier/packing limits. Retain all corpus-dependent
   construction work and measure initialization separately from steady serving.
   Four partitions reduce a constant; they do not make total ANN rebuild cost
   independent of corpus size. The million/billion-event claim remains unproved.
2. Measure sustained open-loop offered writes, explicit queue/deadline failures,
   phase-exclusive serving/commit wait, peak/RSS and retained-index bytes. Same
   completed counts in this closed-loop fixture are not offered-load equivalence.
3. Test visible mutation, adverse concurrency and process kill at publication
   boundaries, followed by recovery and cross-epoch learner invalidation. The
   current complete candidate/200 race run is one workload, not all safety paths.
4. Compare actual public retrieval/rank and complete scored-law outputs against
   the unsharded control; ANN graph/search order need not be identical. Future-ID
   exclusion is not equality under counterfactual future corpus changes. Network
   and untouched outcome-labeled agent tasks still require their original tests.
5. Separately continue the .698246 TV safe-cap/accuracy problem, valid useful
   Anti-Pigeon decisions, adaptive stationary protection and falsification
   selection under equal TOTAL cost. A storage-speed result closes none of those.

Temporary dependency repairs are research patches only. Promotion also needs
durable format compatibility/downgrade policy, upstream review or a maintained
fork policy, and migration planning. Do not modify or restart production as a
side effect of this research goal. All seven whole goals remain OPEN.
