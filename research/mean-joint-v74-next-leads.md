# Leads that do not redefine the seven goals

Written while the frozen V74 cohort is still running. These are conditional
directions, not diagnosed causes, completed implementations or empirical claims.

## Equivalent Work Elimination First

The isolated V75 anchored-prefix fork retains the complete V74 model and raw
journal. It must pass reference, lifecycle, delayed-evidence and numerical tests
before timing. Full-screen equivalence is a further requirement, not supplied
by the small tests. This lead can rescue cost, not a failed quality criterion.

Another exact-computation lead is replacing a first factor at the anchor with
the joint pair factor by its conditional likelihood ratio. For positive old
factor `L1`, multiply the stored rate filter by `L12/L1`, then normalize and
add the log normalizer to the original evidence log. An old zero factor must
also have zero conditional mass; `0/0` cannot be silently treated as evidence.
Unsupported hyperstates remain unsupported. Older-than-anchor pairs still need
replay. This is not implemented or tested here, and cannot be assumed faster
until matched late-pair/tower/full-journal audits and core timing pass.

## Distinguish Rate Change From Hyperstate Change

V74 changes member rates but keeps mean-map and dispersion hyperstates static.
If joint mean learning still fails recovery, do not conclude that a larger static
grid or lower acceptance threshold fixes it. Audit separately whether changing
calibration/noise hyperstates are needed, using one complete evidence/outcome
joint model and matched stationary controls. Other explanations remain live:
likelihood misspecification, sparse measurements, prior sensitivity and harmful
cross-member borrowing. The fully independent arm is an important control.

[Li, Boyd, Smyth and Mandt (NeurIPS 2021)](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/362387494f6be6613daea643a7706a42-Paper.pdf)
investigate inferred changes, posterior broadening and bounded beam hypotheses.
That supplies a research direction, not a correctness or latency guarantee for
EventFrame. A finite implementation would need to specify the changed parameter,
episode clock, transition law and evidence likelihood. Derive history weights
from their joint probability, and compare small histories exhaustively. Do not
copy a normalized within-history branch probability as a cross-history weight
without checking the history-dependent predictive normalizer. Any beam pruning
or projection is approximate and must disclose lost-mass/error accounting.
Late pairs replace factors at the original outcome, not at their arrival time.

## Protection Must Be Operational, Not Self-Certified

[Huang, Ma and Michailidis (UAI 2026)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v337/huang26b.html)
study online calibration using martingale PAC-Bayesian bounds and a gated residual
head under distribution shift. Their assumptions and surrogate optimization are
not automatically met by our finite selective stream. Before adapting the idea,
inspect the theorem, admissible source/target mismatch terms and update filtration.
An estimated target mismatch without coverage is not an Anti-Pigeon certificate.
Keep an independently scored incumbent and test stationary harm/recovery under
the original gates; do not let the learner certify its own reference law.

## Equal Total Cost And External Outcomes Remain Required

For Goal 7, compare acquisition plus nomination, likelihood/selection, filtering,
delayed replay and publication cost, not just 400 matching second requests.
Matched posterior models and cost-budget stopping need independent replay and
nonvacuous future-data forks. A cheaper equivalent query can reduce the cost
disadvantage but cannot establish superiority without a budgeted experiment.

Goals 3, 5 and 6 still require useful error-controlled splits, untouched labeled
agent tasks, and durable loaded freshness/serving. Synthetic mean learning or
constructor allocation cannot substitute for any of those requirements.
No reserved seeds, sealed labels, private sessions, production or paper changes.
