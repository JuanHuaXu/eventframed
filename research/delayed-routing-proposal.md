## Paired experiment to freeze next

Carry forward the economical coherent one-step observer, not exhaustive
lookahead. Compare generic64 with the same arrived-label training data,
conservative routed feedback and role-carry routed feedback. Include immediate
and combined jitter0..31/missing0.2 schedules, with matched latent trajectories
but separate feedback states. Keep v103's interaction and two change directions
for the first integration; then return to the broader v88/member-split harness.

Do not treat the same trajectory under two schedules as independent evidence.
Use distinct predeclared RNG roles for rules, inputs, outcomes, delays and
missingness. Delays and missingness must be independent of outcome magnitude
in this first quality experiment. Store origin, arrival/expiry, original served
forecast, publication, classification and applied update order.

At clock t, release only labels actually due; then expire unresolved records
according to a fixed deadline and fit from arrived evidence only. The initial
16 labels are known. Later full-frame training audits are counted separately
from foreground reads. Sort eligible labels by origin before taking the last
64 or 32; record their origin span and support. Generating an outcome in the
simulator does not make it available to model fitting or policy selection.

Immediate feedback must reproduce v103 on consumed seeds, and both variants
must agree when no label crosses a publication. Preserve full and late Brier,
expected accuracy, acquisition cost, and per-trajectory update coverage. Check
the origin-prefix accounting on every transition and replay every record.

Freeze gain/non-harm criteria before any new outcome inspection. Retention of
204/204 delivered labels is not itself a quality gate. In particular test
whether old-regime losses hurt the carry selector after a change, and whether
the version-specific gate loses too much evidence to protect forecasts under
scarce feedback. A failure should guide a separate research variant, not a
changed seed or a weaker threshold on the same run.

## Return to Anti-Pigeon

The current journal does not authorize an abstraction split or carry losses
across arbitrary changes in role meaning. Before member-split integration,
bind records to the existing dependency/generation identity as well as the
model publication. Reset or invalidate shared-role records when group meaning
changes, while preserving eligible member-local evidence. Test stale shared
records arriving after a split and out-of-order split notifications explicitly.

Then rerun the older delay/missing and member-split outcomes under their actual
criteria. A pass on the two v103 change cases alone cannot replace v88's broader
case requirements or the v80 post-split forecast-gain failure. Real-agent,
independent-generator and persistence-tail requirements remain separate.
