# V70 Prior-Responsiveness Preflight

Recommendation for mathematical investigation, not a confirmed runtime bug or
an approved scientific rescue. V68/V69 use a member-rate prior with .8 mass at
the supplied baseline and .2 on a finite Polya urn rate. That prior is one
possible source of hesitation, alongside model-family mismatch, noisy sparse
observations, shared transfer, and the evidence nomination policy. No unique
causal diagnosis follows from the existing negative stream studies.

Earlier V40 strength2/4 experiments are retained failures. Their finite-grid
means changed with strength, so they were not variance-only tests. V59's
moment anchoring concerned shared tree leaves. This preflight instead compares
the actual local discrete urn prior at identical first moments, retaining the
current control and separating spike removal from strength change. It opens
neither new empirical labels nor the reserved confirmation populations.

## Declared Algebra

For N=20, baseline b, strength s, and spike mass a, let J have the beta-binomial
law generated by N Polya draws with initial counts sb and s(1-b). Let P=b with
probability a and P=J/N otherwise. Then

    E[P] = b
    Var(P) = (1-a) b(1-b) (N+s) / [N(s+1)].

Controls: (a,s)=(.8,1), (0,1), (0,2). No arm is selected from a stream result.
Given Y|P ~ Bernoulli(P) and W=Y xor Bernoulli(eta), set
q=eta+(1-2eta)b. Our Bayes-rule derivation yields

    E[P|W=1]-b = (1-2eta) Var(P)/q,
    E[P|W=0]-b = -(1-2eta) Var(P)/(1-q).

At a truly stationary rate b, the one-observation predictor's excess expected
Brier risk over the correct baseline is

    (1-2eta)^2 Var(P)^2 / [q(1-q)].

These expressions cover one local fixed-noise update, not the shared mixture
or an external truth guarantee. Resetting with hazard h multiplies the response
by 1-h and this particular excess by (1-h)^2. It does not remove the tradeoff.

[Adams and MacKay (2007)](https://arxiv.org/html/0710.3742v1), sections2.1-2.4,
separates the changepoint prior from the observation model. It motivates
checking those assumptions independently; the finite moment/risk derivations
above are ours, not a theorem attributed to their paper.

## Checks And Falsifier

Candidate prior ratios versus independently enumerated urn dynamic programming;
normalization, exact first/second moments, noisy posterior branches and tower
identity; hazards0,1/16,1; shared-Y paired-measurement enumeration; zero-noise
impossible-pair exclusions; nonvacuous rejection of independent-Y double count.
Seven baselines cover .25 through .925, with eta0,.1,.2. No data-set scoring,
performance claim, observed stream advantage or production change.

If broader priors increase stationary risk while improving responsiveness,
simply widening the prior is not a full rescue. A future implementation would
need an explicit, evaluated preservation/selection mechanism rather than a
relaxed harm gate or a promising mean on consumed worlds. All seven whole goals
remain open, including useful valid splits, untouched agent tasks and durable
loaded freshness. Current V69 source and all prior evidence remain unchanged.
